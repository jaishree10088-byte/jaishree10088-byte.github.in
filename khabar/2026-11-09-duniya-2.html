Two Trains Collide In Egypt's Giza Province, Seven Killed And Over 100 Injured

At least seven people were killed and more than 100 injured after two trains collided in Egypt's Giza province, near the city of Cairo.

The injured have been admitted to nearby hospitals. Authorities have begun an investigation into the cause of the accident.
