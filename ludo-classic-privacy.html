Privacy Policy — Ludo Classic

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · App: Ludo Classic (com.sandip.ludoclassic) · Developer: Sandip Kumar Mahto

Ludo Classic is a free, offline board game. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. Ludo Classic does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app works fully offline and requests no permissions on your device (no internet, no location, no contacts, no storage, no microphone, no camera).

2. No Accounts, No Sign-In

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no social features. You play against the computer or with friends on the same device (Pass & Play). No username, email address, or any other identifier is created or stored.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Ludo Classic contains no advertisements and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not use any third-party SDK that collects data.

4. Children

Ludo Classic is a family board game suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

5. Data Storage on Your Device

Game progress and settings (such as your chosen theme or sound preference) are stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Third-Party Links

The app contains no links to external websites or services.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
